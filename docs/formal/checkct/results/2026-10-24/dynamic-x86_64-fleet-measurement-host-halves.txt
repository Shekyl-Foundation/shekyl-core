reading: every half is flat, fixed against fresh and fixed-pair, including red512 on a square's output; the plain fixed-set helio square rows are flat in this build too. What crosses, strongly and only for HelioseleneField, is a CONSTANT operand (the same uniformly random value for all 32 operations of a batch: -44 at 200k, -72 at 1M) and the STRUCTURED operands (sparse, small, dense: -17 to -57), all faster than uniformly random ones. Field25519 is flat for the same classes. The one asymmetry between the two fields that matches this is the representation: dalek's FieldElement is in Montgomery form, so from_repr turns a sparse, small, dense or constant input into a dense residue before any multiplication; HelioseleneField holds the canonical value, so the operand's structure reaches the multiplier and the reduction intact. Mechanism still open (activity-dependent clocking is the fit; the cycles-against-wall-time test decides).
