Method
  dumpbin /disasm of each binary (the release PDB beside it supplies the labels), and dumpbin /unwindinfo for the x64
  function table. A function is one function-table entry [Begin, End); End is exclusive and excludes the int3 padding
  after it. Leaf functions without an entry run from their label to the next label or entry, less trailing int3/nop.
  Instructions are the listing's instruction rows (an address, bytes, and a mnemonic); the byte-continuation rows and
  the padding rows dumpbin prints between functions are not counted.
  Labels alone do not give boundaries here: a function with no public symbol (internal after LTO) runs on under the
  previous label. In shekyl-wallet-rpc, sign_avx2_inner and both ffsamp_fft_inner instances have no symbol, and sit
  under the label of fips205::wots::wots_sign, the preceding public symbol.
  Functions without a symbol are identified by their instruction sequences: two functions are "identical" when every
  instruction matches once these are masked: absolute code addresses, data addresses ([...h]), anonymous data-constant
  names (anon.*), the 17h<hash>E / .llvm.<n> suffixes of symbol names, and a call to ffsamp_fft_inner shown by name
  in one binary and by address in the other.

Reading
  On a CPU with AVX2 (this laptop; the harness prints cpu_avx2=yes) the signature goes through sign_avx2_inner, the
  AVX2 ffsamp_fft_inner and poly_avx2::poly_LDL_fft. Each of these is the same instruction sequence in the harness and
  in shekyl-wallet-rpc.
  On the SSE2 path, ffsamp_fft_inner and poly::poly_LDL_fft are the same in harness_sse2 and in shekyl-wallet-rpc.
  sign_inner itself is not: wallet-rpc's default-feature build inlines it into SigningKey1024::sign, and the
  register allocation and layout differ. The no_avx2 harness measures the standalone sign_inner. A shipped binary on a
  CPU without AVX2 runs the inlined one.
