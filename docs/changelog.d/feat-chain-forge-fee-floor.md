- **Every forged transaction pays the fee the daemon would ask of it, and passes the daemon's pool** (`CHAIN_FORGE.md` §11.4, `CF-R29`). A forged spend paid a flat fixture fee below the relay floor, so a daemon loading a forged chain through its own acceptance path refused its first spend. Each forged transaction (the fill's spends, the plan's spends, the joins and the emission claims) now pays the economy rung of the fee ladder the daemon serves for its block, on the fee lattice, over its own weight. Before its block is mined, the forge offers each block's listing in order to a fresh daemon pool over the chain that block extends, and a refusal fails the forge; every block the forge writes is held to what the pool admitted. A seeded transaction is built once: its weight is measured on a sizing build from the OS's randomness, never listed, so no plan seed signs two messages. The small chain and the hosted shard-fill chain are re-forged, and shard-fill is re-published and re-pinned.
- **`shekyl_tx_weight::converge_fee_by` and `weight_at_fee`**: the wallet's fee loop over any weight function, and a measured weight at another fee, for a shape the weight predictor does not model. **`Transaction::fee`** replaces each copy of the fee match where a `Null` ct pays zero.
