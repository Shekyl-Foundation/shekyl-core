- **A failed submit says where its reservation stands, in three states.** `SubmitError::reservation_after()` in `shekyl-engine-core` returns `ReservationAfterSubmit`: `Held` (`consumer_held`, nothing sent; the caller submits again or discards), `InFlight` (the daemon may have it; the engine holds it until a verdict, and the caller's discard is refused) or `Gone`. It replaces the yes/no `keeps_reservation()`, which could not tell a held reservation from one in flight: the GUI wallet dropped the handle of a held one (an unsaved send among them), leaving its inputs locked with nothing to release them (Copilot on GUI #74). The match is exhaustive, so a variant added later does not compile until its answer is written.
