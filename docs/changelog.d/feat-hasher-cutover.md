- **One owner of every RandomX cache: the daemon's consumers move onto `shekyl-hasher`, and the cache store is deleted** ([`RANDOMX_CACHE_ACTOR.md`](../design/RANDOMX_CACHE_ACTOR.md), HA-R1…HA-R8, §6 step 3). A cache is 256 MiB, and until now it stayed resident until some consumer remembered to release it; four leaks came from that. The node now starts one hasher and stops it last, and nothing else builds, holds or frees a cache: `shekyl-pow-randomx`'s `CacheStore` is gone, with the slot methods on `Substrate`, the ingest's pin lock, window install and branch gate, chain sync's branch and evidence caches, and the sync model's slot-miss protocol (`Proved::NotHeld`, `Command::SeedNotHeld`, `Event::Seeds`). **Every hash is a job on its seed.** The ingest forms each block as a `run` job through one boundary (`shekyl-chain-ingest/src/pow.rs`); a switch lends one cache per run of blocks under one seed, and none for a run whose hashes are all on record; chain sync's header proofs and the evidence check are `lend` jobs; the miner and the RPC's `pow` borrow only a cache that is already warm and build none. A job always gets its cache, inside a hard cap of three. **What stays warm is computed.** The ingest ranks the seeds the node needs (`ranked_seeds`: the window's one or two, then the seed of the most-work chain the window does not cover, a held fork or chain sync's header frontier) and sends the list after every write that can change it; at quiescence the hasher holds exactly that list. A fresh store derives the null seed for genesis and block 0's id for the list, and keeps one cache. **What an operator sees:** `daemon_status` reports `randomx_caches`, the count of live caches, in the part shown to `status` (RPC 4.4), and `shekyl-cli daemon status` prints it with its MiB. `pow` on a seed that is not warm now answers `pow.seed` with "is not warm"; the null seed stops being warm once genesis has connected. **What holds it:** `scripts/ci/check_randomx_cache_owner.sh` holds the identifier `PreparedCache` to the hasher, the verifier, the differential harness and the cost benches, so a field or a path-qualified `Arc` is the same use as `derive`, fails an allowed path that no longer names it, and refuses `CacheStore` anywhere in Rust code. The loopback harness gives each simulated node a hasher of its own and checks the cap on every wait and the quiet state at every stop. The differential harness's concurrent mode hashes through the hasher, and its RSS ceiling is the cap's. Consensus results do not move: the RandomX known-answer vector is asserted through every way the hasher hands out a hash. The live RSS capture with a fork case and the floor-device reading against PB-R1 are the close-out's (step 4).
