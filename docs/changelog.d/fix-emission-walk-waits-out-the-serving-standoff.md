- **The emission walk waits out the serving host's launch standoff** (`shekyl-engine-core`, the live-daemon acceptance lane). The walk starts the persona's serving host through the production start, which first waits a random standoff of up to `DEFAULT_SERVING_LAUNCH_WINDOW` (10 minutes) before it pins its serve set. The walk gave the pin 300 s, so a standoff past that failed the walk after hours of setup. Its wait is now the launch window plus the Tor start.
