- **Daemon: a task that ends on its own stops the daemon, and says why when it happens** (alpha.12 blocker; Rick, 2026-11-10). An ingest service that stopped on a fault left the daemon running: nothing was logged until exit, and the RPC kept answering from the old tip (2026-11-02, a full disk, 34 minutes). The fault is now logged at `error` when it is recorded, and the node has one end watch, `Node::ended()`, that carries the first fatal end of any task it owns with its cause. Chain sync's owner reports there (it had a watch of its own, `sync_ended`), the ingest service is its second producer, and the daemon's run loop waits on that one watch and exits with a failure status. The stop line reads `the ingest service stopped: <cause>; the daemon stops`. Rule 25 gains the coding rule: a long-lived task the node starts reports its end through `Node::ended()`.
