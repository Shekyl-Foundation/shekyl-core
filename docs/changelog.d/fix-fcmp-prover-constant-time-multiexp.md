- **The FCMP++ prover's multiexp no longer leaks its secret scalars through the cache.** multiexp 0.4's non-vartime `multiexp` read its Straus tables and Pippenger buckets at an index taken from the scalar's bits, and the prover passes it secrets: the Pedersen and vector commitments, the AI, AO and S commitments, the t and tau points, and the witness tape's commitments. The prover crates (`generalized-bulletproofs`, `fcmps`, `shekyl-fcmp`, `shekyl-fcmp-proofs`, `shekyl-engine-core`, `shekyl-verify-cost`) now take multiexp 0.5, which reads every entry and keeps the right one with a constant-time select. multiexp is declared once for the workspace, with the `alloc` feature on, because without it 0.5 falls back to a naive sum. Proofs are mathematically unchanged, so their bytes, sizes and verification are too, and the verifiers keep their variable-time paths. The vendored crypto subtree is re-synced from the Foundation's `monero-oxide` fork (PR #8, upstream `497f60b`'s multiexp hunks). An ignored test, `time_sign_transaction_by_shape`, prints what signing a transfer costs at 1, 2 and 8 inputs.
