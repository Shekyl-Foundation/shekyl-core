- **One definition of spendable, the balance's legs, the signer's errors by name, and one fee tier set** (alpha.11 bug hunt §A16–A18, wallet lane PR 2; Rick's rulings of 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-15). The balance counted money as unlocked once the tip reached its eligible height, seven blocks before a build could spend it; now one classifier (`TransferDetails::state`, in `shekyl-engine-state`, calling the curve tree's reference predicate) decides spendable for the balance, the spendable views and the send path alike. `get_balance` (contract 0.19.0) carries `balance = spendable + incoming` and each leg once, with `incoming_wait` (absent when nothing incoming is on chain) and, beside the balance, `outgoing` and `unspendable`; `liquid`, `unlocked` and `pending` are deleted. The wallet has no user-set hold on outputs: the ledger's `frozen` flag and the engine's freeze and thaw are deleted (ledger block version 15, wallet ledger format 26; an older wallet file is refused for its version and names the remedy, restoring from the seed), and `freeze`, `thaw` and `frozen` are refused by name in the CLI and the wallet RPC, with the reason. The signer's errors keep their identity (`SignFault`): `-29123 TOO_MANY_INPUTS`, `-29124 TOO_MANY_OUTPUTS` (refused at the request), `-29125 INPUT_NOT_IN_TREE`, `-29126 INPUT_UNSPENDABLE`, and `-29119 SIGNER_FAILED` naming its fault in `data.fault`; none is reported as a remote failure. The intermittent signing failure the TRC-1 wallet hit (`BalanceSelfCheck`) was the fee variant choice: a leftover just above the with-change fee took the no-change fee and left the rest unaccounted. The fee is now always a ladder value: that leftover becomes a change output of at least one atomic unit, however small, and a leftover exactly equal to the with-change fee, which would leave a change of zero, is never selected. The fee tier is `economy`, `standard` or `high`, lowercase, one type (`shekyl_economics::FeePriority`) in place of five copies; the custom rate is deleted (a rate of its own marks a wallet), with its `-32602` arm, its contract form and the RPC client's Monero-numbered priorities. The fee tier is not a preference: the prefs file's `default_priority` and the fee reply's `default_priority`, which nothing read, are deleted, and a send that names no tier takes `standard`. The prefs schema is 5; an older file is refused for its version, with its remedy, before its body is parsed. A reserve proof discloses every on-chain output in the balance (spendable and incoming).
