- **One definition of spendable, the balance's legs, the signer's errors by name, and one fee tier set** (alpha.11 bug hunt §A16–A18, wallet lane PR 2; Rick's rulings of 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-15). The balance counted money as unlocked once the tip reached its eligible height, seven blocks before a build could spend it; now one classifier (`TransferDetails::state`, in `shekyl-engine-state`, calling the curve tree's reference predicate) decides spendable for the balance, the spendable views and the send path alike. `get_balance` (contract 0.19.0) carries `balance = spendable + incoming + parked` and each leg once, with `incoming_wait` (absent when nothing incoming is on chain) and, beside the balance, `outgoing` and `unspendable`; `liquid`, `unlocked` and `pending` are deleted, and a send only parked money would cover is refused `-29127 OUTPUTS_PARKED`. The signer's errors keep their identity (`SignFault`): `-29123 TOO_MANY_INPUTS`, `-29124 TOO_MANY_OUTPUTS` (refused at the request), `-29125 INPUT_NOT_IN_TREE`, `-29126 INPUT_UNSPENDABLE`, and `-29119 SIGNER_FAILED` naming its fault in `data.fault`; none is reported as a remote failure. The intermittent signing failure the TRC-1 wallet hit (`BalanceSelfCheck`) was the fee variant choice: a leftover just above the with-change fee took the no-change fee and left the rest unaccounted. The fee is now always a ladder value, and that leftover becomes a change output, however small. The fee tier is `economy`, `standard` or `high`, lowercase, one type (`shekyl_economics::FeePriority`) in place of five copies; the custom rate is deleted (a rate of its own marks a wallet), with its `-32602` arm, its contract form and the RPC client's Monero-numbered priorities. The prefs schema is 5 (`default_priority = "standard"`); an older file is refused for its version, with its remedy, before its body is parsed.
