- **A stake is signed when it comes due, not when it is requested** (`shekyl-engine-core`, `shekyl-engine-state`, `shekyl-wallet-rpc`; `ARCHIVAL_BOND_POST_DELAY.md` BD-Q2, BD-Q3, ruled 2026-10-16, and how a plan ends, ruled 2026-11-26). The request now reserves the persona's funding and draws the delay once, measured from the first block the funding can be bonded, and signs nothing. At the due block the wallet fills the shards the bond owes, quotes the fee, signs at the standard reference age of 6 and sends in the same step. A post sent late no longer shows when it was signed, and no post is sent that can no longer land. A plan that cannot be signed waits while the problem is temporary; a fee above its funds, or a node that is not this computer's, waits up to a maximum and then ends. A plan also ends when its funds are spent elsewhere, when the node no longer holds the archive data, or when the owner withdraws it. `unstake` withdraws a stake that is still a plan (wallet RPC 0.26.0: `UnstakeResult` verdict `WITHDRAWN`), and refuses one already signed. A plan that ends sends its funds back to the wallet's own balance and retires its persona; staking again uses a new one. `staking_info` shows a due stake's reason, on a plan and on a post not yet sent, and the outcomes `withdrawn`, `unbondable` and `waited_out`. The unwired funding-to-bond floor (`MIN_COLD_START_SPACING`) is deleted. The pending-post records move to version 15; a wallet with an earlier pending file refuses to open it, pre-genesis.
