- **A frame output is a type, and the signers and proofs over one take it** (`NETWORK_IDENTITY.md` §2.4.1; `DOMAIN_BINDING_RATCHET.tsv`). `frame` returns `Framed32` or `Framed64`, which only the envelope can make. The emission auths (Auth-B and Auth-P), the witness bundle signature, the message-signing outer Schnorr, the outbound and inbound tx proofs, the reserve proof and its DLEQ take the frame output by type: `sign_emission_claim` and `sign_emission_backing_for_output` sign a `&Framed64`, the outer Schnorr signs `preimage ‖ σ_pq` from the framed preimage, and `prove_dleq` / `verify_dleq` take a `&Framed32`. Every hashed and signed byte is unchanged.
- **RPC channel: the mac1 key derives from the daemon's bundle and a busy reply answers a proved mac1.** `Mac1Key::of(&StaticPublic)` applies `shekyl/rpc-mac1-v1`. A `Mac1Tag` is made only by the mac1 computation or handed back by the daemon's mac1 check, and `shekyl/rpc-busy-v1` is applied to one. The two registry rows now name those inputs (the static bundle and the mac1 tag, not a Noise message), and both labels live in `mac1.rs`. The wire is unchanged.
- **The domain-binding ratchet's `input-raw` section goes from 17 rows to 7.** Ten rows are typed. Attestation and receipt (2) go with SO-D12 4b. The five `SaltForKey` rows wait on rulings: their notes say what each site's input is.
