- **Relay privacy: an embargo cannot fire before a transaction could have come back** (`DAEMON_RELAY_PRIVACY.md` §100; Rick, 2026-11-22). The embargo on a stemmed transaction was a purely random wait with no minimum, and since the change that made a node's own transaction draw the same embargo as a relayed one, a short draw could fire while the broadcast was still on its way back. The node then re-sent the transaction along its second path and, on a node with few peers, did not recognise the broadcast when it arrived by that path, so it went on listing its own transaction as unbroadcast and left it out of its own block template until the next timer fire. The network had the transaction throughout. Every embargo now waits at least the time a stem of ordinary length takes to go out and its broadcast takes to come back along a single path, 36.5 s on clearnet and 57 s on Tor, and the random wait is added to that. The figure is derived from the existing stem, hop and broadcast-delay parameters, and is the same for a node's own transactions and the ones it relays, on every node. Times read from the timer are later by it: the mean embargo is 230 s on clearnet and 568 s on Tor (194 s and 511 s), the age at which the daemon reports an unpublished own transaction overdue is 2,466 s (2,352 s), and the wallet's failed-send wait is 2,409 s (2,352 s).
