- **Relay privacy: an embargo cannot fire before a transaction could have come back** (`DAEMON_RELAY_PRIVACY.md` §100; Rick, 2026-11-22). The embargo on a stemmed transaction was a purely random wait with no minimum, and since the change that made a node's own transaction draw the same embargo as a relayed one, about one own transaction in a hundred drew a wait of a few seconds. The node then re-sent the transaction along its second path while the first was completing, and did not recognise the broadcast when it came back, so it went on listing its own transaction as unbroadcast and left it out of its own block template until the next timer fire. The network had the transaction throughout. Every embargo now waits at least the time a stem of ordinary length and its broadcast take to return, 5.5 s on clearnet and 22.75 s on Tor, and the random wait is added to that. The figure is derived from the existing stem and broadcast-return parameters and is the same for a node's own transactions and the ones it relays. Times read from the timer are later by it: the mean embargo is 199 s on clearnet and 534 s on Tor (194 s and 511 s), the age at which the daemon reports an unpublished own transaction overdue is 2,398 s (2,352 s), and the wallet's failed-send wait is 2,375 s (2,352 s). The chance that a stem completes before any timer on it fires rises from 0.90 to 0.998 on clearnet and 0.994 on Tor.
