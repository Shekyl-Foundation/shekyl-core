- **Test chains can carry regtest's network identity** (`CHAIN_FORGE.md` §6, `CF-1a`). The scenario driver opens a chain held to regtest's table genesis, connected as block 0 through the node's own genesis assembly, and pays a miner derived through the wallet's account derivation under regtest's name. The harness spender signs and proves under the chain's network id instead of a fixed harness id, harness personas can be derived under a named network, and the emission-claim and bundle assemblies take the chain's id. A new test holds a regtest chain to four locks: another network refuses its fixed difficulty, CEN-E1 refuses its genesis, CEN-I18 and the FCMP++ verifier refuse its transactions under every other network's id, and the transport's network-derived prefix keeps it off other networks' wire. Chains that mine their own genesis keep the harness id until their consumers migrate.
