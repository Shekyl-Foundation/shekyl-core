- **The fleet's shekyld units do not restart after an operator's forced stop** (Rick, 2026-11-21). `utils/fleet/skl-node@.service` and `skl-clearnet@.service` restarted on failure and counted exit 130, a second stop, as one; they now carry `SuccessExitStatus=130` as the packaged unit does since #1490, and the launch-recipe gate holds every unit that restarts `shekyld` to it. Fleet hosts pick this up when their units are redeployed.
