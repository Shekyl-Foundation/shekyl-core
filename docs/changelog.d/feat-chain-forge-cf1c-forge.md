- **A test chain is forged from a plan, and comes out the same bytes on every machine** (`CHAIN_FORGE.md` `CF-1c`). The new `shekyl-chain-forge` crate holds the plan format, the seed derivation, and a forged chain's manifest and block ledger (`blocks.txt`). Its runnable is a cargo example that builds every block through the production template, validator and store on a chain carrying regtest's identity, writes the corpus as blocks land, and produces the trace by replaying the written corpus under the production proof-of-work. Every source of randomness a block reaches (range proofs, blinds, membership proofs, the emission's backing proof, the post-quantum signatures, the witness's draw commitment, bundle and receipt signatures, the shard fill's parallel spends) draws from the plan's seed through a test-only seam. CI forges the small plan twice in two processes that differ in thread count, working directory, time zone and locale, and holds both to the committed ledger and manifest; an aarch64 leg does the same nightly and on changes to the crypto beneath a chain. For each source, a run with that one source drawn from the OS must differ from the committed chain at the first block that source reaches.
- **A forged chain is regtest's alone, and a tampered receipt is refused where a block is connected.** On the forged small plan: its levers are refused under every other network, its block 0 is refused under their genesis pins, and every transaction it lists is refused under their ids, a re-signed spend, emission claim and bond post each at its proof row. A block carrying a bundle with one field changed is refused at the row that field reaches first, and the same block untampered connects.
- **The bundle producer can run reproducibly under test.** Its witness ring and its task's randomness take a caller's draws behind test-only features, so a producer plan forges the same bytes in two processes. The plan names the spread its reads start on, since the producer draws its start delays from the stream its read plans come from. A producer run drawing everything from the OS, its reads on another spread, settles as the planned run: every archival table and field is held, except three fields that record when a draw became visible (a block's shortfall and its reduction, and an issued draw's reveal height), and those are held by timing-free forms instead (each epoch's total reduction and closing shortfall, and every reveal inside its response window). Each block's draw count and carry are held at every height.
- **The alt-branch store walks use ordered collections,** so no hash seed can reach a row they write or a reply they give.
- **`shekyl-chain-replay baseline` runs library code** (`rebaseline::baseline`), the same function the forge produces its traces with.
