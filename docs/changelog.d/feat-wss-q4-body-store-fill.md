- **A staker's persona body store is filled before its bond is posted** (`WALLET_SIDE_STORE.md` `WSS-Q4`, `WSS-Q15`…`WSS-Q18`).
  - **The fill.** For each shard the bond owes, the wallet reads the shard's blocks from its own node over loopback, pins both ends of the walk to what the persona scan verified, reads every member transaction's whole body, checks each against its txid, frames the shard and writes it to the body store in one transaction. A reply that does not match the verified chain writes nothing.
  - **Fill first, as a type.** A bond post is assembled only from holdings that a completed fill returned. `stake` fills before anything durable is written. A fill that cannot complete answers one of seven new codes, `-29538`…`-29544`, each naming its remedy, among them a shard the node has already discarded, which cannot be filled until a node reads below its horizon. A `CompleteTree` bond owes every closed and final shard, and its post waits for the persona scan to reach the finality horizon (`-29538 STAKE_SCAN_BEHIND`).
  - **The daemon reports a block's cumulative archival length** (`block_show`, `block_list`: `archival_cumulative`, RPC 4.8, IG-Q112). The persona scan checks each block's total against the lengths its own transactions' txids bind, from genesis, and seals where each shard closed and a digest of its rows.
  - **The reconcile and its alarm.** At each open, each settlement epoch and after a fill, the wallet checks that every shard the bond owes is held and its stored body verifies, refills what is lost from its own node, and pulls each newly final shard in its freeze epoch. `shard_alarm` (wallet RPC) and `shard alarm` (CLI) list what was lost this session and what the refill did; nothing is stored.
  - **Serving.** A 404 for a shard the persona does not hold moves its own counter, `not_held`, which raises no alarm. Serving's caught-up check compares the bond record's height with the wallet's synced height instead of the curve tree's ingest tip.
  - **Formats.** The persona scan's cursor moves to version 3 and its state to version 12; a staking scan file from an earlier build is refused at open with `-29530`'s remedy: move that one file aside, and the scan rebuilds it from the chain.
