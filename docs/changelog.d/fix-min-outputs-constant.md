- **A transaction's minimum output count is one named constant** (Rick, 2026-11-22). `shekyl_wire::transaction::MIN_NON_COINBASE_OUTPUTS`, two, sits beside `MAX_OUTPUTS`. Consensus row CEN-I1 and the wire's two pre-checks read it in place of a literal 2. Its comment says which forms it covers: every non-coinbase transaction (spends, bond posts and emission claims alike); the coinbase's count is CEN-F4's.
  - **The builder refuses fewer outputs than consensus admits,** in `sign_transaction` and in `BuiltSpend::build`, so it no longer makes a one-output transaction that every node refuses. No send path made one: transfers always carry change, and sweeps, bond posts and claims build two or three outputs. The refusal is its own fault, `too-few-outputs` on `-29119 SIGNER_FAILED` (wallet RPC contract 0.23.0; `api_version` stays 1).
  - **Test fixtures that signed one output now sign two,** including the weight table's measurement cell, whose FCMP++ proof sizes do not depend on the output count.
