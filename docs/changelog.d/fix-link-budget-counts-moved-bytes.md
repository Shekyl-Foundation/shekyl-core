- **P2P: the link budget counts what moved, and a quiet connection holds none of it** (`p2p-upload-max`, `p2p-download-max`). A connection's download and upload figures in `peer_connections` and the node's totals in `p2p_stats` are the bytes that actually reached or left the socket. Before, a reader took its share of the download budget before its peer had sent anything, so an idle connection's download figure ran up to 8 KiB ahead of what had arrived, and under a download limit each idle connection held part of the budget that active connections could not use. A read is now counted when its bytes land and paid for before the next read; a write is counted as the socket takes it.
