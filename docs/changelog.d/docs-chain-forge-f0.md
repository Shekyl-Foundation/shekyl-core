- **Design: the chain forge and the canonical test chains (Round 0).** `docs/design/CHAIN_FORGE.md` (moved from `docs/CANONICAL_CHAINS.md`) designs one tool that forges the chains every lane tests against, from a catalogue of what lanes need: Chain L for the archival lifecycle, P for the production schedule under load, W for live LWMA difficulty, and B for the bundle producer once the forge skeleton shows what the producer depends on besides chain state. Each regenerates byte for byte at a commit, carries only regtest's network identity, and has its seeded randomness confined to test-only features. The document holds the census of consumers, the event catalogue, the determinism contract and its seed seams, corpus layout 4 with alternative branches, the snapshot cache, and the regeneration and storage policy. Chain bytes up to 5 MB are committed; larger chains commit their manifest, block-hash list and trace and pin the bytes as a release asset, which is a cache of a regeneration at the pinned commit. The lane's rulings are in the decision log; its identifier family `CF-` is registered in the implementation index. No code changes.
