- **Design: the chain forge and the canonical test chains (Round 0).** `docs/design/CHAIN_FORGE.md` (moved from `docs/CANONICAL_CHAINS.md`) designs one tool that forges the chains every lane tests against, from a catalogue of what lanes need: Chain L for the archival lifecycle, P for the production schedule under load, W for live LWMA difficulty, and B for the bundle producer once the forge skeleton shows what the producer depends on besides chain state. Each regenerates byte for byte at a commit, carries only regtest's network identity, and has its seeded randomness confined to test-only features. The document holds the census of consumers, the event catalogue, the determinism contract and its seed seams, corpus layout 4 with alternative branches, the snapshot cache, and the regeneration and storage policy. Chain bytes up to 5 MB are committed; larger chains are OCI artifacts on GHCR, pinned by digest in a committed pin file, published only through a reviewed, approved workflow, and treated as a cache of a regeneration at the pinned commit; each chain's plan and block-hash list stay in shekyl-core. A gate refuses seeded random-number generators in production code except at sites listed one by one, each with the source of its seed. The lane's rulings are in the decision log; its identifier family `CF-` is registered in the implementation index. No code changes.
