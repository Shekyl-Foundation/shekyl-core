- **The path-assembly bench's replay-window plan pairs on its rung, not on the window** (CT-6, ruled 2026-11-09). `assemble_edge`'s same-rung pair is the window rung's floor and 1.5× that floor, as on every shape rung. The replay window is a reading beside that rung — timed and recorded as its own arm, not a pair role — and its separation from the floor is the printed leaf ratio, never a stored field and never a criterion. An unconverged window does not withhold the pair grade. At `S = 3` the window sits 1.24× above its floor, too close for a pair to separate a slope from noise. The bench records move to schema version 4, because a version-3 `ReplayWindow` record's `rung_top` was the window itself.
