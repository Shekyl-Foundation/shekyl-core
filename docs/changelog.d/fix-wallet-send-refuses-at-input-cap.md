- **A send that needs more inputs than one transaction spends is refused by name.** A wallet holding many small outputs, such as a miner's coinbases, that asked to send more than its largest eight outputs cover had its selection handed to the membership-proof step whole, which refused it as a curve-tree outage and suggested a rescan. The wallet now refuses at selection, before reserving anything, with `-29123 TOO_MANY_INPUTS`: the number of inputs the send needs, the limit of eight, and the remedy (send less, or first send part of the balance to yourself).
