- **Daemon RPC 4.1: a reply holds the fields its request selected, and `daemon_status` follows its grants** (Rick, 2026-11-12; `RPC_WIRE.md` RW-Q4 as amended, `RPC_CHANNEL.md` §6.1). **Breaking for every daemon RPC client:** a client refuses a daemon of another version at the version read.
  - **`pow` and `prune` select fields.** A header carries `pow`, a hash and never `null`, exactly when the request asked for it; a header the daemon cannot supply it for is refused. A transaction's entry in `tx_show`, `tx_list`, `block_show` and `block_list` is `{id, body}` with the whole body, and under `prune` the skeleton: the pruned body with `prunable_hash`, `pqc_auth_hash` and `archival_size`. A client refuses a reply that carries a field its request did not select, or lacks one it did.
  - **`daemon_status` by grant.** A caller holding `health` is told whether the node is usable now: the tip, the target, the sync and offline flags, `peered`, the writer, the protocol version and its grants. The chain's facts need `chain`, and `txpool_count` needs `pool`; a caller without the grant finds them absent. The view listener holds both, so a wallet sees no change.
  - **`shekyl-cli`** says which grant a connection lacks when a console word needs a chain fact it is not shown.
