- **The RPC channel listener, the node's key and enrolment** (`RPC_CHANNEL.md` RT-W10 step 4; `INPUT_GRAMMAR.md` IG-Q87, IG-Q88). `rpc-listen` serves the authenticated Noise channel on any address, `none` by default. The node makes its key on its first start, in its data directory, readable by its account alone, and refuses to start with a key anyone else can read. Clients are admitted by fingerprint: an unenrolled one gets no answer past the third message, an enrolled one is told its ceiling and holds the grant it asked for within it. Five methods under `enrol` manage the enrolled set at `CORE_RPC_VERSION` 4.4: `rpc_client_add`, `rpc_client_set`, `rpc_client_remove`, `rpc_client_list` and `rpc_bundle_show`; removing a client, or lowering its ceiling, closes its live sessions. A service has a console key, replaced offline with `shekyld --rpc-console-key-reset`.
- **A service's data home is fixed per platform** (Rick, 2026-11-14): `/var/lib/shekyl` on Linux, `/var/db/shekyl` on FreeBSD and macOS, `%ProgramData%\shekyl` on Windows. The daemon checks it and never creates it (`config.service.root`). The packaged unit sets `StateDirectoryMode=0700` and passes no `--data-dir`. On Windows `rpc-socket-dir` is refused (`config.socket-dir.platform`).
- **Messages 3 and 4 of the channel's handshake are 16 bytes each** (RT-O31): a version and the grant bits; anything else is refused. The independent oracle's vectors are regenerated with those payloads.
