- **The CLI's `--json` is the daemon's reply, and its lines say block numbers.** `chain`, `shard list all`, `shard show` and `mine status` print the daemon's typed reply as the wire serializes it, so their `--json` has no names of its own: `chain --json` no longer puts the block count under `height` (the wire's name for the tip's own number), and the 3.x names `wide_difficulty`, `outgoing_connections_count` and `incoming_connections_count`, and the `p2p` string, are gone. A part the connection's grant withholds is absent, as on the wire. `shard list all --json` no longer writes an unestimated pay as `0`, and `mine status --json` carries the reply's `block_spacing`, `height`, `reward`, `cause` and `txpool_retired`. `mine start` and `mine stop` keep their own output. What people read says "block" and a block's own number, never "height" (`HEIGHT_SEMANTICS.md` §1): `wallet` and `status` show "Synced to block", "Daemon block" and "Restore from block", so a synced wallet shows its daemon's block, and a wallet that has scanned nothing says so; `chain` shows "Block number" and, while syncing, "Target block"; the staking scan, staked outputs, transfers, `tx show` and the daemon console name blocks, and the console's progress lines say "Block count". Typed names (options, command words, wire and contract fields) do not change. `RELEASING.md` gains the alpha resync step, `GS-C5` (the chain-wide staking figures) is registered, and `daemon_height`'s description cites `chain_height`, not the retired `get_height`.
