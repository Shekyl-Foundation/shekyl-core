- **Multisig (Option E′): the live ceremony's primitives** (slice 3, PR-A; `PQC_MULTISIG.md` §12.3). A member signs its input of a multisig spend with `sign_multisig_member_auth`, which recovers the member's per-output key from its own `0x0D` ciphertext, signs the input's signed hash under the multisig domain, and wipes the key in the same call; the key derivation is no longer public. `SalSigningParams::propose` takes the spent output's commitment mask and the input's pseudo-out blind, so a multisig spend's pseudo-outs balance as a solo spend's do.
- **Multisig ceremony blobs are sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305 and a random 24-byte nonce** (Round 0 ruling R-4), under a key `shekyl-crypto-pq` expands from the group transport secret for one intent, message type and sender (`multisig_blob`, label `shekyl/multisig-blob-key-v1`). A nonce derived from a persisted counter could repeat after a restore from backup. `shekyl-multisig`'s own AEAD module, its counter-derived nonce and its unlabelled key derivation are deleted.
- **A spend intent holds at most 8 inputs**, the consensus cap, where it allowed 128; `validate_structural` now refuses an intent with no input, too many inputs or recipients, or an over-long address.
