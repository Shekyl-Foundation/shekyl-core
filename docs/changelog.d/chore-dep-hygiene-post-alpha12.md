- **The chain store runs on redb 4.6, and an unsound dependency fails the audit.** redb 4.6 stops a repair from opening a damaged store as an empty one, refuses to commit a transaction whose table was leaked, and no longer returns a later commit's data to a read that was in progress at close; the file format is unchanged. The store creates its files through `create_with_backend`, which `create_file` was already doing. postcard is built without `heapless-cas` and imageproc 0.27 without its defaults, which takes heapless 0.7 (nine unsound advisories, RUSTSEC-2026-0574..0582), paste, ttf-parser and atomic-polyfill out of every lock (the maintained `pastey` comes in as a build-time macro); the shard rasters are pixel-identical. The workspace and fuzz locks are refreshed (rustix 1.1.6 clears RUSTSEC-2026-0486). The crates on the proving, signing, wiping and RNG paths are held at their earlier versions until they are reviewed (`docs/FOLLOWUPS.md`). `cargo audit` now fails on an "unsound" advisory as it does on a vulnerability, and its three discharged "unmaintained" ignores are gone.
