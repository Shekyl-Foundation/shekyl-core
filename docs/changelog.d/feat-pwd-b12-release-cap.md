- **A peer can no longer make a session hold 100 MB with one transaction notice or an unfinished fragment train** (`PWD-B12`). Before, a `NOTIFY_NEW_TRANSACTIONS` was accepted up to the 100 MB packet limit, a relay flush sent a peer's whole queue as one notice however large, and a fragmented message was checked only when its last fragment arrived.
- **A transaction notice is now at most 1 000 044 bytes, on both ends.** That is the size of a notice carrying one transaction of the largest size a transaction may be (1 000 000 bytes), so a single transaction always fits. A relay flush fills one notice with whole transactions up to that size, in the order they arrived, and sends the rest on the next flush; a node refuses a larger notice at its header and closes the session.
- **A fragmented message is checked at its first fragment.** Its inner header is read as soon as it has arrived; an inner message that is too large for its command, that is not a request or a response, or whose fragments keep coming after the whole message is in, closes the session, each with its own cause in the log (`InnerMessageOversize`, `FragmentTrainOverrun`, `InnerMessageNotDispatch`). Not yet bounded: how much a node's relay queue holds for a peer, which waits on per-connection rate limiting (`PWD-B1`).
