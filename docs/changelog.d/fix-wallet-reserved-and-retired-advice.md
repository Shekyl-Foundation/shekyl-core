- **Wallet RPC: an in-flight staking entry says how much it holds** (`WALLET_RPC_STAKING_OUTCOMES.md` WR-Q6, ruled 2026-11-07; `wallet_rpc.yaml` 0.16.0). Every `staking_info` `in_flight` entry carries a required `reserved`: the amount its reserved inputs still hold, `"0"` when nothing is held, and `null` until the staking scan has run, because until then the amount cannot be read. The published schema is a union of that decimal string and null, which is what OpenAPI 3.1 accepts. `shekyl-cli`'s `stake` says the same on each line. A refused or expired zero-fee reward claim is no longer said to hold funds: the engine's `refused` reason drops "Its funds stay set aside", and the CLI says funds return at the end date only for a record that holds some. A record that holds nothing, or whose amount is not yet known, is said to stay until the scan passes its end height, and neither line says the scan will show whether it was mined.
- **A wallet file from before this release is refused without wrong advice.** The refusal used to say "restore the wallet from its 24 seed words", which fails for exactly those files: a 64-character seed is refused, and 24 recovery words open a different, empty wallet. It now says the backup will not bring the wallet back and to create a new wallet. `shekyl-cli` and `shekyl-wallet-rpc` print it in their logs.
