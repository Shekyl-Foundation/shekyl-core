- **The owning connection: end of stream means the daemon is gone** (`INPUT_GRAMMAR.md` IG-Q106; Rick, 2026-11-17). `shekyld` holds a second handle on its owning connection until its main returns, so the process that started it reads end of stream when the daemon has finished stopping (rendezvous removed, data directory unlocked), not when the stop began. A half-close by the starting process stops the daemon as a close does. A launcher starts its next daemon on that read, with no timer and no kill. Test: `a_half_close_stops_it_and_end_of_stream_means_it_is_gone`. `scripts/ci/gui_wallet_core_deps.toml` gains `shekyl-spawn` and `shekyl-tor-status`, the GUI wallet's direct edges for its own launch of the daemon (GS-2) and its Tor status display (GS-3).
- **Each Tor fix has its own words** (`shekyl-tor-status`; Rick, 2026-11-17). `fix_sentence` gives one fix's sentence, and the status line's fixes are those sentences joined in the reply's order, so a client that offers the fixes one by one (the GUI wallet's Settings) says each as the line does.
- **A send is not read as address-hiding over a Tor link that carries nothing** (`shekyl-tor-status`; Rick, 2026-11-17). Under a managed Tor, `TorStatus::own_tx_hides_address` also requires `carriage` to be `carries` or `open-only`; `gone` and an unknown carriage fail closed. The accessor and the status line now agree over every origination and carriage, and a table test holds them to it.
- **One classification of where the node's own transactions go** (`shekyl-tor-status`; Rick, 2026-11-17). `TorStatus::own_tx_fate` returns `OwnTxFate` (`Hidden`, `Held`, `OpenNetwork`, `Offline`, `Unknown`), failing closed; `own_tx_hides_address` is the fate being `Hidden`, and the status line's first sentence is picked from the fate. A reply with a `tor` or `clearnet` setting this version does not know, or an origination that needs Tor beside `tor = off`, now renders the update sentence alone where it rendered a held sentence before it.
- **A Tor report that contradicts itself is said to** (`shekyl-tor-status`; Rick, 2026-11-17). When every keyword in the reply is known and they do not fit together, so no fate can be read (Tor off beside an origination that needs Tor), the status line says "The node's Tor report contradicts itself, so nothing can be said about where its transactions go." The update sentence is kept for a keyword this version does not know.
