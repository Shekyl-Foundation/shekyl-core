- **A persistent onion keeps its second-layer guards** (`TOR_SUPERVISOR.md` §4.4 and `TSR-W1`, closing `TSR-Q15`; `INPUT_GRAMMAR.md` IG-Q114). With `tor-onion-key-file`, the daemon now pins the onion's second-layer guards and keeps them across restarts, rotating each at the end of its lifetime, instead of drawing new ones at every start. It uses the guard manager the persona's Tor uses, now shared code. The onion is not published until its guards are pinned on its Tor. When they cannot be, `daemon_status` says why with the onion causes `guards-refused` (a fault to report; asked again at the supervisor's backoff cap) or `guards-unfilled` (it waits for Tor's view of the network), and `CORE_RPC_VERSION` is 4.10. A rotation that fails later keeps the onion serving on the guards Tor holds, and the log says so. A guard that expired while the machine slept is replaced at the wake. A persistent onion is now suitable with clearnet off.
