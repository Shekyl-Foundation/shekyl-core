- **A Tor supervisor that ends on its own stops the daemon, and the exit is a failure** (rule 25). A supervisor that could not start (the node's timing engine refused it, or the system's random source refused its onion key or its probe's isolation), or whose loop returned or panicked, used to leave the daemon running: with `tor = managed` and clearnet off it had no network, and every `daemon_status` answered "not ready". The daemon now stops through its normal teardown, says why and what to do, and exits failed. Under `tor = off` or an operator's Tor the supervisor mints no onion key or probe isolation, so a failure there can no longer end it.
