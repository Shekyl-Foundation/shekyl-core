- **The live-daemon tests' P-scan wait is bounded by progress** (`shekyl-engine-core`). A wait had a fixed length (300 s, or 1,800 s and 3,600 s where a caller set its own), but its work is the scan's backlog. The emission walk's reward discovery had about 5,900 blocks to scan, covered 4,896 of them in 300 s, and failed after 16 hours. A wait now fails only when no new height is sealed for 300 s, and it prints the scan's rate in blocks per second on success and on failure, so a slow scan stays visible.
