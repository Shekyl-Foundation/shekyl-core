- **Public items that only their own tests used are gone or narrowed.** `shekyl-consensus` keeps only the two output lock windows: its `ConsensusProof` trait, `ConsensusRegistry`, `RandomXProof` stub, `BlockHeader`/`ChainState` types and `ConsensusError` had no caller outside the crate, and rule 70 forbids a proof trait or registry without an accepted second proof type. `shekyl_wire::tx_extra::tag_carries_payload` (no caller; `TxExtraField::payload` is the typed form) and `shekyl_archival_retention::settlement_epoch_slash_deadline_height` (its one reader, a test, now calls `SettlementSchedule::slash_deadline_height`) are deleted. `checked_relay_fee_floor` and `checked_corrected_fee_ladder` are crate-private; the typed refusal stays public through `relay_floor_from` and `fee_ladder_from`. No behaviour changes.
