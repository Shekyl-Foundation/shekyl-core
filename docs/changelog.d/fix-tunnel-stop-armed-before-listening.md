- **`shekyl-rpc-tunnel` stops cleanly on a stop signal from the moment it says it is listening.** A SIGTERM or Ctrl-C that arrived just after "Listening on" ended the tunnel by the signal itself and left its socket file behind; a service manager stopping a tunnel right after its start, or a start-and-stop script, saw an unclean death. A stop signal landing between two waits of the accept loop could also be lost, so the tunnel did not stop. The stop signals are now registered once, before the listener is bound, and held for the tunnel's life; a registration that fails is a start error.
