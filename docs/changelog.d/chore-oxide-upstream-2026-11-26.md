- **The FCMP++ prover builds its divisors with upstream's corrected code.** The vendored crypto takes monero-oxide's 2026-11-26 fixes through the Shekyl fork (PR #12). The Barycentric divisor calculator is gone: its division by batch-inverted evaluations left a zero denominator as zero, which could hand the prover a wrong divisor and an invalid proof. Divisors now come from the rewritten `Poly` code, whose storage zeroizes. A divisor that cannot be normalized is no longer a panic but its own prover error, and the error says which blind failed. A fresh blind's failure clears by proving again, which draws new blinds. A PQC leaf blind's failure means that output cannot be spent, because its blind is the opening of the output's on-chain commitment. Blinds are single-use and lose `Clone`, except the PQC leaf blind, which is reused by design and documented as such. The SAL multisig zeroizes its state on drop. The verifier computes no divisor and is unchanged.
