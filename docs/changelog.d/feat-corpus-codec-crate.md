- **The corpus format has its own crate, `shekyl-corpus`** (`CF-4a` PR 2, Rick's ruling of 2026-11-18; `docs/design/CORPUS_CODEC.md`). Its magic, layout version 4, `CorpusWriter`, `CorpusReader` and `CorpusFault` moved out of `shekyl-chain-ingest`, unchanged in behaviour, so the chain store's tests can read a forged chain's corpus without depending on the ingest crate (which depends on the store). The reader now yields `Record::Extend` or `Record::Rewind`, and the ingest's replay `Source` is `CorpusSource`, a thin adapter over it; callers import the writer and the fault from `shekyl-corpus`. Every committed corpus (the five replay vectors and the nine bench fixtures) decodes and re-encodes from its parsed records byte for byte. The crate is in the release graph through `shekyl-chain-ingest`, joins the rustdoc `-D warnings` gate and the inland-height gate's scope; the height-typing ratchet drops to 122 with the record encoder's height typed.
