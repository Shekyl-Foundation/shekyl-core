- **Economics sim: the pool is the one the Rust daemon holds** (ESR-17; `ECONOMICS_SIM_PRODUCTION_REBASE.md` §5.27). A waiting transaction keeps the fee and weight it was built with, leaves when its reference leaves CEN-I11's window (94 blocks after it was built), and is held under CEN-M10's limit, `REFERENCE_BLOCK_MAX_AGE` × the block weight limit, with admission evicting only strictly cheaper entries; the template offers by fee per weight, then the hour received. The sim calls the pool's own policy: `shekyl-mempool` gains a default `pool` feature, and without it the crate is `policy` alone (the fee rate, both orders, the limit, `make_room` and `trim_to`), which the pool also calls; CI lints and tests the crate without `pool`, the build the sim links. `--stage2` prints expired, evicted and refused transactions per block, the waiting depth and the age at inclusion. Since a waiting body keeps its build weight, the stuffer's miner chooses the honest bodies it drops per weight, cheapest first within a weight and every split across weights, not as one cheapest-per-byte prefix. On the baseline the pool is cap-bound in the constrained era (about 4.3 k waiting, about 2 arrivals a block refused) and serves the oldest first (median age 86 blocks); a backlog no longer survives to be served later. The dead C++ define `CRYPTONOTE_MEMPOOL_TX_LIVETIME` is deleted: the sim's pin test was its last reader. `ArchivalParams` refuses a section budget multiple at or past `W₂`, and the full pair's 1/16 acceptance is the named constant `FULL_PAIR_ACCEPT_MASK`. The year-1 Standard fee ceiling is ratified at 0.05 % of the year-1 block reward, about 0.496 SKL (`ECONOMY_UMBRELLA_PLAN.md` §4).
